About Parent Club

Less lecture, more conversation.

Parent Club is run by parents, for parents — supportive, honest antenatal classes for expecting families across South Manchester.

Sarah Bond

Our story

Founded by two mothers.

Parent Club was started by two mums who felt antenatal preparation should be warmer, more honest and rooted in real community. We run small, supportive classes built around evidence-based knowledge, practical guidance and genuine emotional support.

We're based in Levenshulme and welcome expecting parents from across South Manchester, including Burnage and Stockport, into a calm space where every question is welcome.
