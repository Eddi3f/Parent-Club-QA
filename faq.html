FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things expecting parents most often ask us. Can't see yours? Just get in touch.

When should I book an antenatal course?
Most parents join us in their second or early third trimester, roughly between 24 and 34 weeks. That said, it's never too early to reserve a place — our small classes fill up quickly.
Where are the classes held?
All of our in-person sessions run in Levenshulme, South Manchester, and we welcome parents from across Burnage, Stockport and the surrounding areas.
Are the Pregnancy Socials really free?
Yes — our Pregnancy Socials at The Sidings are free to attend for pregnant people and their partners, friends or relatives. First-time pregnant people even receive a free goody bag.
Can my partner or birth supporter come along?
Absolutely — our classes are designed for expecting parents and their birth partners together. The price covers both of you.
What if I can't make an evening course?
We also run intensive weekend courses that cover everything in a single focused weekend, ideal if weeknights don't work for you.
Do you offer any free resources?
Yes — sign up on our contact page and we'll send you a free birth plan template and hospital bag packing list.
